The tools

Start with the two jobs every desk does all day

TOOL 01

PNR Decoder

Paste a raw GDS itinerary — Sabre-style segment lines — and get back a clean, readable itinerary: airline, flight, route, date, times and booking status, line by line.

  • Reads segment status codes (HK, KK, SS, HL, NN and the rest) and names what they mean
  • Flags any line it can't parse instead of guessing
  • Works from the same segment-line format your GDS prints in an *I display
Live demo · runs in your browser

TOOL 02

Name Checker

Compare the name as booked against the name on the passport or ID. Catches reordering, dropped middle names, and the one-letter typos that cause denied boarding or a rejected e-visa.

  • Ignores titles (MR/MRS/MS) and the GDS LAST/FIRST order automatically
  • Tells a harmless missing middle name apart from a real mismatch
  • Flags likely typos (one or two letters off) separately from a clean match
Live demo · runs in your browser
