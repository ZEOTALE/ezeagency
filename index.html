TOOL 02

Name Checker

Compare the name as booked against the name on the passport or ID. Catches reordering, dropped middle names, and the one-letter typos that cause denied boarding or a rejected e-visa.

  • Ignores titles (MR/MRS/MS) and the GDS LAST/FIRST order automatically
  • Tells a harmless missing middle name apart from a real mismatch
  • Flags likely typos (one or two letters off) separately from a clean match
